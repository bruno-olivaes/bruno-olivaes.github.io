The Five Mandatory Integrity Gates

To eliminate publication bias, noise-mining, and false-positive claims across all experimental datasets, candidate periodicities must simultaneously satisfy five independent statistical filters:

Gate 1: Resolvability

Fundamental frequency must fall strictly within the instrument Rayleigh and Nyquist bounds (ωmin ≤ ω ≤ ωmax).

Gate 2: Amplitude

Best-fit sinusoidal amplitude must exceed twice the empirical RMS noise floor (|A| > 2.0 × RMS).

Gate 3: Precision

Asymptotic standard error of the scaling factor must not exceed 20% (σλ / λ < 0.20).

Gate 4: Multiplicity

Phase-scrambled bootstrap-of-maximum p-value must pass pcorrected < 0.05, neutralizing the look-elsewhere effect.

Gate 5: Information

Bayesian Information Criterion penalty must favor the oscillatory model (ΔBIC = BICnull - BICDSI > 10.0).
